# 🎥 Project Feedback Video Script
## Learner Demographics and Course Enrollment Behavior Analysis on EduPro

### Duration: 3-5 minutes
### Recording Tool: Use Loom (free) at https://www.loom.com or OBS Studio

---

## INTRO (30 seconds)

> "Hi, I'm Navin D. In this video, I'll walk you through my project —
> Learner Demographics and Course Enrollment Behavior Analysis on EduPro.
> This project was built as part of the Unified Mentor Internship Program.
> It analyzes 3,000 learners, 50 courses, and nearly 7,000 enrollment
> transactions to provide data-driven insights for the EduPro platform."

---

## SECTION 1: Overview Tab (45 seconds)

*[Show the Overview tab on screen]*

> "The dashboard opens with key KPIs at the top — we can see the total
> number of learners, enrollments, courses offered, and the average
> courses per learner. The gender ratio shows a nearly balanced platform.
>
> Below, the age histogram shows our learners range from 15 to 35 years old,
> and the pie chart confirms a healthy gender balance."

---

## SECTION 2: Learner Demographics Tab (45 seconds)

*[Click to Demographics tab]*

> "In the demographics tab, we break down age groups by gender.
> The 18-25 group is our largest segment. The stacked bar chart shows
> gender is fairly evenly distributed across all age bands.
> The summary statistics table gives us the mean age and spread."

---

## SECTION 3: Enrollment Analysis Tab (45 seconds)

*[Click to Enrollment tab]*

> "Here we see Technology and Business are the most popular course categories.
> The pie chart shows the split between Free and Paid courses.
> Beginner-level courses dominate enrollments, which makes sense for an
> online learning platform. The monthly trend shows consistent enrollment
> throughout 2023 and 2024."

---

## SECTION 4: Cross-Analysis Tab (30 seconds)

*[Click to Demographics × Course Preferences tab]*

> "The heatmap is one of the most insightful visuals — it shows which
> age groups prefer which course categories. We can see the 18-25 group
> has the highest enrollments across Technology and Business.
> Gender differences in course selection are minimal."

---

## SECTION 5: Behavioral Insights Tab (30 seconds)

*[Click to Behavioral Insights tab]*

> "Finally, the behavioral insights show most learners take 1-3 courses.
> Our top 10 most active learners are power users. The course level
> breakdown by age group shows younger learners prefer beginner courses."

---

## SECTION 6: Filters Demo (15 seconds)

*[Show sidebar filters in action]*

> "The sidebar lets us filter by age group, gender, course category,
> and level — all charts update in real-time."

---

## CLOSING (30 seconds)

> "Key learnings from this project:
> 1. I strengthened my skills in Python, Pandas, and Plotly
> 2. I learned to build interactive Streamlit dashboards
> 3. I understood the importance of descriptive analytics before
>    jumping to predictions
>
> Thank you for watching! The source code is available on my GitHub."

---

## RECORDING TIPS:
1. Open the Streamlit app in Chrome (full screen)
2. Use Loom's screen + camera recording
3. Speak clearly and slowly
4. Click through each tab as you narrate
5. Upload to YouTube as "Unlisted"
